# The Returning Stain

### Reactive - Utility

**Novice Rank**

*The skin barely has time to become clean.*

| Range | Area | Duration | Roll |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon reach | 1 Creature | Instantaneous | A.R. |

| Saving Roll | Impact | Rhythm | Attrition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | — | 3 | 2 |

## Requirements

- Novice or higher competency with the weapon used.
- A weapon with the Corrosion profile.
- A known residue application procedure, with its requirements met and material available for a fresh application. If it comes from a Technique, you must know that Technique.

## Keywords

- Corrosion

## Effect

When a creature declares an action to remove an external chemical residue from a target within your weapon's reach, you may declare this Technique before that action resolves. The target may clean itself or receive another creature's help.

Resolve the removal normally first. Then make an A.R. against the target's D.R. to apply the same residue again at the same affected point. You must be able to reach the target and meet the application requirements at that moment. Keep the original procedure's specific modifiers; a residue already removed does not penalize this new defense.

If your A.R. does not exceed the target's D.R., you do not reapply the residue. If it does, resolve the fresh application with the original procedure's resistances and residual effects. Its severity, persistence, consumption and removal follow that procedure; you do not copy the previous applicator's competency.

This Technique deals no damage and applies no weapon effects. If the previous residue remains, you neither stack its penalties nor automatically increase its severity. Pay this reaction's cost, not the originating Technique's activation cost as well. The removal action retains its costs and result even if you fail to reapply.

You may attempt this Technique only once per removal action. You cannot use it against treatments for internal poisons, infections or wounds, repairs of material damage, or removal caused solely by the environment.

## Example

You know Blur the Signal and have its Ammunition Kit and material for another application. An enemy attempts to clean the residue from its visual channel using Interact. Declare The Returning Stain and resolve the cleanup first. Then make your A.R. with Blur the Signal's −2 penalty. If you exceed the enemy's D.R., apply the residue again to that channel with the severity corresponding to your own competency. If you tie or fail, you do not reapply it. If the cleanup removed the residue, the enemy is no longer Blinded by that previous application when defending.
