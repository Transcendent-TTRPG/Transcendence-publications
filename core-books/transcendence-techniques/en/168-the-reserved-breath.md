# The Reserved Breath

### Reactive - Utility

**Novice Rank**

*The dust meets a closed mouth.*

| Range | Area | Duration | Roll |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal | You | Instantaneous | S.R. (Swimming) |

| Saving Roll | Impact | Rhythm | Attrition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | — | 0 | 1 |

## Requirements

- Swimming at Novice rank or higher.
- Poison Resistance at Novice rank or higher.

## Keywords

- Swimming
- Poison Resistance

## Effect

When you perceive an imminent, discrete exposure to an inhaled poison, you can react before inhaling it and before resolving its R.R. You do not need to identify the compound, but you must perceive the threat.

Make a Swimming S.R. The Narrator sets the difficulty before the roll according to the speed of the exposure and the circumstances that make controlling your breathing difficult.

On success, you prevent that application by inhalation. On failure, resolve the exposure normally, including its corresponding R.R.

You can attempt this once against each application and pay Attrition even if you fail. This does not prevent other entry routes, remove active poisons or protect against Suffocation. It cannot be used against continuous exposure and grants no protection against later applications.

## Example

During a fight, you see an enemy release a burst of poisonous powder toward you. Before inhaling it, declare the Technique; the Narrator sets the difficulty and you make the Swimming S.R. On success, you prevent that application by inhalation. On failure, resolve the normal exposure and R.R. In either case, gain 1 Attrition. If the compound also acts through contact, resolve that route normally.
