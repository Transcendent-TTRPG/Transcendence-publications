# The Unnoticed Gesture

### Active - Utility

**Novice Rank**

*The buckle closes beneath the crunch of bones.*

| Range | Area | Duration | Roll |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal | You | Instantaneous | S.R. (Stealth) |

| Saving Roll | Impact | Rhythm | Attrition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | — | 3 | 1 |

## Requirements

- Novice or higher Stealth specialization.
- Be Hidden from at least one observer.

## Keywords

- Stealth
- Hidden

## Effect

Declare one brief interaction you can perform through Interact, such as drawing or stowing an accessible object, adjusting equipment, picking up a loose object or operating a simple latch. You must meet its ordinary requirements and have a physical way to reduce the signals of its execution. The Narrator determines which signals you can control and declares the difficulty before your roll.

Make a Stealth S.R. against that difficulty and perform the interaction as part of this Technique. If you fail, the interaction occurs, but its signals compromise Hidden under the normal detection rules. Failure does not automatically reveal your position.

On success, the controlled signals of that execution do not generate a provoked detection opportunity for observers from whom you were already Hidden. Keep the active Hidden value without rerolling or replacing it. Observers who had already located you do not lose that information.

Visible changes to the object, alarms, light and other signals you have not controlled resolve normally. You do not negate deliberate searches, special senses or the loss of physical support for Hidden.

This Technique's cost includes the interaction; do not also pay for Interact. Pay Rhythm and Attrition even if Stealth fails. This Technique grants no attack, displacement or additional interaction, and does not replace tasks requiring another specialization. Its protection ends with that execution.

## Example

Your Hidden value is 12 and you draw a tool. If Stealth succeeds, its controlled signals do not provoke detection; Hidden remains 12.

If you fail, you still draw the tool. An enemy with a valid detection opportunity rolls Perception: a result of 10 does not locate your position; a result of 13 does.
