# The Lost Dose

### Reactive - Utility

**Novice Rank**

*The edge arrives alone.*

| Range | Area | Duration | Roll |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1m | 1 Surface or Object | Instantaneous | S.R. (Alchemy) |

| Saving Roll | Impact | Rhythm | Attrition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | — | 3 | 1 |

## Requirements

- Novice or higher competency in Alchemy.
- One prepared neutralizer dose ready to apply, compatible with the poison type and of equal or higher quality than the preparation you intend to neutralize.

## Keywords

- Alchemy

## Effect

When an attack is made with a weapon carrying an external poison preparation of a type you know, you can intervene before resolving defense. The poisoned point on the weapon must be within 1 meter of you and physically accessible to the neutralizer. You can intervene even if the attack targets another creature. This Technique grants no displacement or projectile interception.

Choose one external poison dose and make an Alchemy S.R. opposed to the attacker's A.R. If your result does not exceed theirs, the preparation remains intact. If it does, you neutralize the chosen dose, which loses its ability to poison.

For this external application you use Alchemy, not Medicine, and do not need a Medical Kit. This is not an attempt to treat an organism.

In either case, the attack retains its A.R. and continues with its normal defense, impact and other effects. You do not damage or disarm the weapon. You do not neutralize poisons already inside an organism or disable glands or internal secretions. A new dose applied to the weapon is not neutralized by this use.

You can attempt this Technique once against each attack. Consume one neutralizer dose and pay Rhythm and Attrition even if you fail. Poison-handling and self-contamination rules still apply.

## Example

The attacker rolls A.R. 10 and you roll Alchemy S.R. 12: you neutralize the dose, but the attack retains A.R. 10 against its recipient's defense. With a result of 10, the tie would leave the poison intact. In either case you consume the neutralizer and pay 3/1.
