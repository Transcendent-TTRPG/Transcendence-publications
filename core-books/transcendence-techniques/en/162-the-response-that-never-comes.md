# The Response That Never Comes

### Reactive - Utility

**Novice Rank**

*The hand is still trying to arrive.*

| Range | Area | Duration | Roll |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon reach | 1 Creature | Instantaneous | `A.R.` |

| Saving Roll | Impact | Rhythm | Attrition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | — | `4` | `1` |

## Requirements

- Novice or higher competency with the weapon used.
- A weapon with the `Interruption` profile.
- The target must have the active Poison `Entorpecido`, at Minor, Moderate, or Severe severity. You do not need to have applied it.

## Keywords

- `Interruption`
- `Entorpecido`

## Effect

When a target within your weapon's reach declares a `Reaction` that includes a physical attack or movement, you may declare this Technique before that Reaction begins resolving. Make an `A.R.` with your weapon, opposed by the target's `D.R.`. You may intervene even when their Reaction responds to another creature's action.

If your `A.R.` exceeds their `D.R.`, cancel that entire Reaction. The target retains the Rhythm and Attrition costs of the canceled Reaction. If you do not exceed their `D.R.`, the Reaction resolves normally. This Technique deals no damage, applies no weapon effect, and does not modify Entorpecido.

You may attempt this Technique only once against each declared Reaction. You cannot use it against another execution of this same Technique, an Active Action, or an ordinary `D.R.`.

## Example

An enemy with Minor Entorpecido declares a Reaction attack against your companion. They are within reach of your Interruption-profile weapon. You declare this Technique before their Reaction resolves. Your `A.R.` is 12 and their `D.R.` is 10: their Reaction is canceled, they retain its costs, and this Technique deals them no damage. If both rolls were 10, their Reaction would proceed normally.

Entorpecido is the specific Poison defined in the Spanish chapter 11, **Venenos**; its name is retained because an authoritative English translation is not yet defined. Other Ailments, wounds, or Fatigue do not fulfill this requirement.
